VAULT RUNNERS

Draw. Combo. Loot. Survive three bosses.

How to play

  • Attack cards deal damage. Playing several in the same turn stacks a combo multiplier — chain them for big hits.
  • Block cards reduce the boss's next attack. Block resets at the start of your turn.
  • Loot cards give gold on the spot — a few instead let you spend gold mid-fight for a big, risky payoff (a gold cost shows as a yellow badge on the card).
  • Skill cards are utility — some draw extra cards, some give extra energy. The bigger ones ask you to discard a card first.
  • Watch the boss's intent to know what's coming and plan your block.
  • Beat a boss → visit the market → buy cards / trinkets / healing → next boss.
